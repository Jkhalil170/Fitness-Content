# Food graphics and font

Fifteen original realistic food-cutout graphics generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this Reel. Isolated and cropped into separate transparent PNGs from the original generated asset sheet. These are realistic illustrations generated for the edit, not downloaded camera photographs or copies of reference assets. No third-party paid API was called. No Commons food photographs from earlier research are used in the finished video.

Bebas Neue font: SIL Open Font License 1.1, included as OFL.txt. GSAP locally bundled from the existing HyperFrames workflow. User supplied camera footage retained unchanged in framing and natural appearance.
